# The Oddity Orchard — World & Mini-Game Ideas

Status: **Concepts only — not implemented.** Saved October 2026.

## Creative direction
An enchanted, slightly overgrown pistachio orchard with a rustic fairy curiosity shop. Keep the existing painterly exterior: mountains, flowers, winding path, wooden sign, black cat. Correct oversized pistachios in trees and bucket. Replace pottery-looking shop displays with wooden jewelry racks, small antique glass cases, drawers, jars, mushrooms, crystals, botanicals, tiny curiosities. Avoid modern boutique styling.

## Interior concept (future scene)
First-person, adult eye-level viewpoint, standing at the entrance of the shop, facing inward. Immersive full-screen landscape illustration, no dollhouse/isometric viewpoint. Mobile should preserve natural landscape proportions and begin at the center, allowing horizontal touch swiping; desktop can pan with mouse/trackpad. Potential interactive hotspots on jewelry, keychains and the shop counter.

## Hidden-object mini-game — "The Gremlin's Treasure Hunt" (working title)
- The shop doubles as a seek-and-find game: visitors look for a small set of hidden trinkets throughout the illustrated room.
- Hidden items can be decorative, not products: brass key, little mushroom, glass bottle, cat figurine, etc.
- Clicking/tapping a correct hidden item marks it as found in a small checklist and can reveal a playful pop-up, optionally featuring a real handmade product.
- Once all items are found, show a celebratory completion screen and optionally unlock a "Gremlin's Treasure Wheel" reward experience.
- Potential rewards: a discount code or non-monetary surprise. Prize values, odds, redemption limits, expiration, eligibility, fraud prevention, and applicable promotion rules must be decided before launch. Prefer no-purchase-necessary participation.
- Prioritize accessible interaction: tappable targets, keyboard support, text alternatives/hints, reduced-motion setting, and a way to enjoy the shop without playing.
- Game should be optional; browsing products and accessing Etsy must never require completing it.
- Mobile: horizontally pannable wide scene with centered initial viewport. Distinguish dragging/panning from tapping hotspots.
- Do not embed a reusable coupon code directly in client-side source as a supposed secure secret; plan validation/redemption separately.
